# 🖼️ Asset Documentation: Wallpapers

This project utilizes high-quality third-party wallpapers to enhance the user's experience

---

### 🛠️ Naming Convention
To maintain a clean filesystem and ensure compatibility with modern shell environments like `fish`, all files must adhere to the following strict format:

> **Format:** `{AuthorAlias}-{ArtName}-{Mods}.jxl`
> **Resolution:** `3840 X 2160 Pixels or greater, aspect ratio may be different`

* **ArtName:** Must be written in **PascalCase**
* **AuthorAlias:** Correlate with the registry below
* **Mods:** Modifications made to the original image from the creator, their modifications from the source this project derives do not apply here unless they contact this project to change something, at the position of filename
0 within a position, universally means that respective modification or any of its iterations were not performed

### 1. Resolutional
1 - Image was upscaled or expanded without the use of AI
A - Image was upscaled with the use of AI
2 - Image was downscaled or cropped without the use of AI
B - Image was downscaled with the use of AI

### 2. Content modification
1 - Media was censored/blurred with black stripes or even gaussian blur
2 - Media was simply blurred, with specific tweaks, color pallete modification for effects
3 - Image certain parts were replaced with AI modifications

* **File Format:** Strictly **JXL** (JPEG XL)
* **Whitespace:** Zero spaces allowed in the filename
